What arrives, and when

The Signal North intelligence brief is sent every Monday at 6:00 a.m. Eastern, before the week’s board meetings and bid deadlines. It runs to roughly a thousand words and is written to be read once, carefully.

Each edition reads the week’s public record and reports what moved:

01
Tenders and awards
Every solicitation, amendment and contract award issued by police services, fire departments and the agencies that buy on their behalf.
02
Boards and councils
Police board agendas, minutes and budget items, plus the council votes that decide whether they proceed.
03
Legislature and ministry
Hansard, estimates, ministry announcements and the regulatory changes that follow them.
 

Nothing else will be sent to this address. No product announcements, no sequences, no invitations to webinars.

Members reach the record underneath the brief: every tender, award and agenda item we read, filed by service and by date, with the documents attached. If that would be useful to your work, reply to this message and we will explain what it covers.
