WHAT ARRIVES, AND WHEN

The Signal North intelligence brief is sent every Monday at 6:00 a.m. Eastern, before
the week's board meetings and bid deadlines. It runs to roughly a
thousand words and is written to be read once, carefully.

Each edition reads the week's public record and reports what moved:

01  TENDERS AND AWARDS
    Every solicitation, amendment and contract award issued by police
    services, fire departments and the agencies that buy on their behalf.

02  BOARDS AND COUNCILS
    Police board agendas, minutes and budget items, plus the council
    votes that decide whether they proceed.

03  LEGISLATURE AND MINISTRY
    Hansard, estimates, ministry announcements and the regulatory
    changes that follow them.

------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing else will be sent to this address. No product announcements, no
sequences, no invitations to webinars.
