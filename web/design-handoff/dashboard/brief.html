Halton's camera refresh moves to its decisive quarter.

Lead item

Halton Regional Police confirmed in June that its digital-evidence licence ends 31 March 2027, and the July board agenda carries the staff report on replacement options. On the intervals we have measured across comparable services, the tender window is February to March 2027, unlikely to slip past June.

Editor's note · G. Da-Ré

The board's June language reads like a service that has already decided to compete this rather than extend. Watch the September agenda for the terms of reference.
